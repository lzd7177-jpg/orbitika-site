Sudoku: A New Puzzle Every Time, in Easy, Medium and Hard, Offline

7 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Sudoku in Orbitika is a free 9x9 puzzle game with a new puzzle each time and three difficulties: Easy (36 clues), Medium (28) and Hard (22). Tap a square, tap a number, use Check to see how many of your entries are right, and New for a fresh puzzle. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Sudoku is the perfect travel puzzle. It needs no words, no signal and no equipment, and a single grid can last the whole flight or disappear in the time it takes a kettle to boil. The only thing you need is a never-ending supply of new puzzles.

That’s what the Sudoku in Orbitika gives you. Every time you press New, the game builds a brand-new puzzle on your phone.

What it does

You open the game (the title bar says SUDOKU) and see three difficulty buttons, the 9x9 grid, a row of number buttons from 1 to 9 and three action buttons: Clear, Check and New.

  • Tap a square to select it. Squares that came with the puzzle are fixed and can’t be changed.
  • Tap a number to put it in the selected square.
  • Clear empties the selected square.

Three difficulties

  • Easy: 36 numbers given.
  • Medium: 28 numbers given.
  • Hard: 22 numbers given.

The fewer clues, the more you have to work out. Switching difficulty starts a new puzzle at that level.

Check

Tap Check to see how you’re doing. The game tells you either that all the numbers you’ve entered are correct, or how many of your entries are right out of the total, for example “6 out of 8 numbers are correct”. If you haven’t entered anything yet, it says so. The message fades after a few seconds. When you finish the puzzle, you get a congratulations message and a strong vibration.

A new puzzle each time

The puzzles aren’t stored in the app: a complete valid grid is generated each time, then numbers are removed to leave the clues for your chosen difficulty. That’s why it never runs out.

Why it’s offline

The puzzles are generated on your phone, so Sudoku is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It works on a plane, a train or a camping trip.

Who it helps

Puzzle fans, commuters, anyone on a long journey, and people who like to keep their minds busy without a screen full of notifications.

A few tips

  • Start with the numbers that appear most. If a digit is already in many places, find its remaining spots first.
  • Scan rows, columns and boxes. The square with the fewest possibilities is the best place to start.
  • Use Check sparingly. It’s more satisfying to finish without it.
  • Start on Easy. Move up once you’re finishing puzzles comfortably.

An honest limit

This is a straightforward Sudoku. There are no pencil marks or notes, no hints, no timer and no undo, and the game isn’t saved if you leave it. Check compares your entries with the puzzle’s stored answer, so treat it as a guide while you’re solving.

Frequently asked questions

What difficulties does Sudoku have?

Three: Easy with 36 clues, Medium with 28 and Hard with 22.

Do I get a new puzzle every time?

Yes. Each puzzle is generated on your phone when you start or tap New.

What does the Check button do?

It tells you whether the numbers you have entered are correct, or how many of them are right.

Are there hints or pencil marks?

No. There are no hints, notes or undo, only Clear for the selected square.

Does Sudoku work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
