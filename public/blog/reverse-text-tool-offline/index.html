Reverse Text: Type Anything and See It Backwards Instantly, Offline

4 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Reverse Text is a free tool inside Orbitika. Type or paste any text and it appears backwards immediately in a box below, with a character count. Tap Copy to copy the result or Clear to start again. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Reversing text is one of those small, silly tricks that’s surprisingly useful. Kids love secret messages, puzzle makers use backwards clues and palindrome fans check their favourite words. And sometimes you simply want to see how something looks when it’s flipped.

I added Reverse Text to Orbitika as a tiny tool with no extra steps: type, and the backwards version is already there.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says REVERSE TEXT) and see an input box with the hint “Type something…”, an arrow, and a dark output box underneath. As you type, the reversed text appears in the output box straight away, in bright cyan letters, with no button to press. Until you type, the output box says “Reversed text will appear here”.

  • Character count: a counter in the corner shows the length, such as “11 chars”, once you’ve typed something.
  • Copy: the green COPY button copies the reversed text and shows a “Copied!” message. It’s disabled until there’s something to copy.
  • Clear: the CLEAR button empties the input and the output together.
  • Selectable output: you can also press and hold the result to select part of it.

An example

Type “Hello Orbitika” and you get “akitibrO olleH”. Type a palindrome such as “level” and you get “level” back, which makes it a quick way to check one.

Why it’s offline

The flipping happens on your phone as you type, so Reverse Text is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Nothing is sent anywhere, which also means your text stays on your phone.

Who it helps

Puzzle and escape-room makers, teachers and students, people writing playful messages or captions, game players making usernames, and anyone curious about palindromes.

A few tips

  • Paste a whole paragraph. The output box scrolls, so long text works too.
  • Reverse it twice to check. Copy the result, paste it back in and you should get the original text.
  • Use it for secret notes. Share the backwards text and let friends use the tool to decode it.
  • Combine it with other word tools. Try Decision Maker to choose which message to flip.

An honest limit

Reverse Text flips the text character by character, so it works best with ordinary letters, numbers and punctuation. Emoji and some combined characters, such as letters with separate accent marks, are made of more than one piece internally and can come out scrambled when reversed. It also reverses letters only, not words, so it won’t turn a sentence into “word order backwards”.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reverse text in Orbitika?

Type or paste your text into the input box. The reversed version appears below as you type.

Can I copy the result?

Yes. Tap the green Copy button. It is available once there is text to copy.

Does it work with emoji?

Not reliably. Emoji and some combined characters can come out scrambled because they are made of more than one piece.

Does Reverse Text work offline?

Yes. It runs on your phone and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Is Reverse Text free?

Yes. It is one of the free tools in Orbitika.
