Lucky Numbers: A Random Lottery Number Picker for 16 Countries, Offline

1 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Lucky Numbers is a free tool inside Orbitika. Choose one of 16 countries and tap Generate Numbers, and it picks random, non-repeating numbers in that country’s range, sorted from lowest to highest, plus the bonus number or numbers. It is for fun only, and it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Every group of friends has the same conversation before a big jackpot: “What numbers are you picking?” Somebody uses birthdays, somebody uses the same numbers every week, and somebody says, “Just let the computer choose.” That last person is the reason I built Lucky Numbers.

It’s a very small tool: pick a country, tap one button, get a set of numbers. It doesn’t predict anything, and I’ll be clear about that further down. It’s there for the fun of the ritual.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says LUCKY NUMBERS) and see a country selector with a flag, a summary line such as “6 numbers · Max: 40”, and a big GENERATE NUMBERS button. Tap it and, after a very short pause, a row of numbers appears with the bonus number or numbers marked separately. A line at the bottom says “Random numbers · No internet needed · Offline”.

16 countries and their settings

Each country has its own count and range, so the numbers you get fit that country’s style of game:

  • New Zealand: 6 numbers from 1 to 40, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 10 (this is the opening country).
  • Australia: 6 numbers from 1 to 45, plus 2 bonus numbers from 1 to 45.
  • United Kingdom: 6 numbers from 1 to 59, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 59.
  • United States: 5 numbers from 1 to 69, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 26.
  • Canada, Germany, Singapore and Malaysia: 6 numbers from 1 to 49, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 49.
  • France: 5 numbers from 1 to 49, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 10.
  • Spain: 5 numbers from 1 to 54, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 10.
  • Italy: 6 numbers from 1 to 90, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 90.
  • Japan: 6 numbers from 1 to 43, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 43.
  • Philippines: 6 numbers from 1 to 58, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 58.
  • India: 6 numbers from 1 to 50, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 50.
  • South Africa: 6 numbers from 1 to 52, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 52.
  • Brazil: 6 numbers from 1 to 60, plus 1 bonus from 1 to 60.

How the numbers are chosen

The main numbers are drawn at random from the country’s range, and the same number can’t appear twice in the main set. They’re then sorted from lowest to highest so they’re easy to copy onto a slip. The bonus numbers are drawn the same way, within their own range.

Why it’s offline

The numbers are generated on your phone, so Lucky Numbers is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s nothing to download and no draw results to fetch, which also means it never needs to know who you are.

Who it helps

People who like a harmless ritual before a draw, groups doing an office pool, anyone who’s tired of picking the same birthday numbers, and teachers who want a quick random-number example for a probability lesson.

A few tips

  • Treat it as a way to avoid bias, not a way to win. Humans pick birthdays, so numbers above 31 are used less often.
  • Tap again for a new set. Each press produces a fresh, independent draw.
  • Check your country’s rules. Make sure the game you play uses the same count and range.
  • Set a budget before you play. If it stops being fun, step away.

An honest limit

Lucky Numbers is for entertainment. It cannot predict results, and every valid combination has exactly the same chance of being drawn, whether it came from this tool, a birthday or a hunch. The settings are my own for each country, and real games change their rules, so check the official game’s numbers and ranges before you play. It also isn’t connected to any lottery operator and doesn’t show past results.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries does Lucky Numbers support?

Sixteen: New Zealand, Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, India, South Africa and Brazil.

Can the same number appear twice?

No. The main numbers never repeat within a set, and they are sorted from lowest to highest.

Does it improve my chances of winning?

No. Every valid combination has the same chance. The tool is for fun only.

Does Lucky Numbers work offline?

Yes. The numbers are generated on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Is it connected to a lottery?

No. It is not affiliated with any lottery operator, and you should check the official game’s rules before you play.
