Snake: The Classic Nokia-Style Game That Speeds Up as You Grow

7 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Snake in Orbitika is the classic Nokia-style game, free and offline. Steer with the on-screen arrows, eat the food to grow and score, and avoid running into yourself. The snake gets faster as your score rises, and it wraps around the edges of the grid instead of stopping at a wall. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

There’s a good chance Snake was the first game you ever played on a phone. A tiny green screen, a handful of pixels and a mounting sense of panic as the tail got longer. It was brilliant because it was simple, and because it never needed anything but a few buttons.

I put Snake into Orbitika for exactly that reason. It’s the classic, with a retro look and nothing in the way.

What it does

You open the game (the title bar says SNAKE) and see a score panel with SCORE and BEST, the playing field and a set of arrow buttons. A start prompt waits until you’re ready. Once you begin, the snake moves by itself and you steer it up, down, left or right.

  • Eat the food to grow longer and add a point.
  • Don’t hit your own body. That’s the only way the game ends.
  • Pause at any time and resume from the same place.

The field and the walls

The playing field is a grid 20 squares wide and 18 high. There are no walls to crash into: if the snake goes off one edge, it comes back in from the opposite side. That makes the game more forgiving at first and more tangled later, when your body fills the screen.

It gets faster

The snake starts at a gentle pace, one move every quarter of a second. Each point you score makes it a little quicker, down to a limit of about 80 milliseconds per move. By the time your score is in the dozens, the snake is flying. Reversing straight back on yourself isn’t allowed, so you can’t turn around by accident.

Game over

When the snake runs into itself, the phone gives a strong vibration and a game-over message appears with a restart button. Your best score for the session stays on the panel, so there’s always a number to beat.

Why it’s offline

Snake runs on your phone, so it’s designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It’s tiny, which suits older phones: no downloads, no ads and nothing to wait for.

Who it helps

Anyone who misses the Nokia era, commuters, people waiting in queues, and anyone who wants a game they can start and stop in a minute.

A few tips

  • Use the edges. Because the field wraps around, an edge isn’t a danger. Plan routes that cross it.
  • Keep the snake in long loops. Tight turns trap you as your tail grows.
  • Look ahead. The faster it gets, the more you need to decide a couple of moves early.
  • Take a break. Use pause if you’re interrupted.

An honest limit

Snake is a simple game with a single mode. There are no levels, no obstacles and no online leaderboard, and the best score is tracked for the session only, so it starts again when you leave the game.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the snake?

With the on-screen arrow buttons: up, down, left and right. You can also pause the game.

Do the walls kill the snake?

No. The field wraps around, so the snake comes back in from the opposite edge. The game ends only when it hits its own body.

Does Snake get faster?

Yes. It starts at one move every 250 milliseconds and speeds up with each point, to a limit of about 80 milliseconds.

Is my best score saved?

The best score is tracked for the session. It starts again when you leave the game.

Does Snake work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
