Date Difference: Count the Days (or Just the Workdays) Between Two Dates, Offline

24 October 2026 · updated 29 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Date Difference is a free tool inside Orbitika. Pick a start date and an end date, choose whether to count only workdays (Monday to Friday), and tap Calculate Days. You get the number of days between the dates, or the workdays plus the total. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

How many days until the holiday? How long is my notice period? How many working days do I have before the deadline? These questions sound simple, and then you try to count across a month-end and a weekend and lose track around the third month. I’ve done it on my fingers more times than I’d like to admit.

That’s why I built Date Difference. It does one thing: you give it two dates and it tells you how many days are between them, either as a plain count or as workdays only. It takes a few seconds and it’s the same answer every time.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says DATE DIFFERENCE) and see two date cards, Start Date and End Date. Tap either one to open a date picker, which covers the years 2000 to 2100. Dates are shown as day/month/year, so 05/11/2026 means 5 November 2026. The tool opens with today as the start date and a week from today as the end date, so the first answer is already one tap away.

Under the dates there’s a SWAP DATES button, a Workdays Only box and a big CALCULATE DAYS button. Until you calculate, the result panel says “Pick your dates and tap the button above”.

Two ways to count

  • Plain days: with the box unticked, the result is the number of days between the two dates, for example “7 days” for today to a week from today.
  • Workdays only (Mon-Fri): tick the box and you get the number of Monday-to-Friday days, followed by the total, like “6 workdays (Mon-Fri), Total days: 7”.

Here’s a real example. Counting from 29 October 2026 to Christmas Day, 25 December 2026, the tool gives 57 days in total and 42 workdays.

How the numbers are worked out

This is the part worth knowing, because the two answers count slightly differently:

  • The total days figure is the gap between the two dates. Monday to Friday of the same week is 4 days.
  • The workdays figure counts every Monday-to-Friday date from the start date to the end date, including both of them. The same Monday-to-Friday example gives 5 workdays.

The tool compares dates only, not times of day, so daylight saving changes can’t make an answer one day short.

The small helpers

  • Swap dates: flips start and end in one tap if you picked them the wrong way round.
  • A clear warning: if the start date is after the end date, the tool says “Start date must be before end date” instead of showing a negative number.
  • Fresh result every time: change a date, swap them or toggle workdays and the old result clears, so you never read a stale answer.

Why it’s offline

Date Difference is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It’s simple date arithmetic done on the phone, so there’s nothing to load, which suits planning on a plane or a train.

Who it helps

Anyone counting down to a trip, a wedding, a birthday or an exam; people working out a notice period, a trial period or a return window; freelancers estimating delivery times; and students planning how many working days are left before a deadline.

A few tips

  • Check the date format. It’s day/month/year, which matters for dates like 03/04.
  • Use workdays for deadlines and plain days for countdowns. A countdown to a holiday cares about every day, a project cares about working ones.
  • Add or remove one if your rule is inclusive. Some situations count both the first and last day. Plain days gives you the gap, so add one if both days count.
  • Use Swap when in doubt. It’s faster than reopening both pickers.

An honest limit

The workdays count is Monday to Friday only. It doesn’t know about public holidays, so a deadline that spans one will have one more workday than you really have. In the Christmas example above, 25 December is counted as a workday because it falls on a Friday. The picker also stops at the year 2100 and starts at 2000.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count the days between two dates?

Pick a start date and an end date, then tap Calculate Days. The result shows the number of days between them.

Can it count only workdays?

Yes. Tick Workdays Only (Mon-Fri) and the result shows the workdays and the total days.

Does it subtract public holidays?

No. It counts Monday to Friday and does not know about public holidays.

What date format does it use?

Day/month/year, for example 05/11/2026 for 5 November 2026. The picker covers 2000 to 2100.

Does Date Difference work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
