Unit Converter: 10 Categories and 62 Units, Offline and Instant

24 October 2026 · updated 1 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Unit Converter in Orbitika is a free tool with 10 categories and 62 units: length, weight, temperature, speed, area, volume, time, digital storage, energy and pressure. Pick a category, choose From and To units, type a value or tap a quick value, and the result updates instantly. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

You’re in a kitchen with a recipe in cups and a scale in grams. Or you’re in a country where the road signs are in kilometres and the car’s speedometer says miles. Or the weather forecast says 68 and you have no idea whether to bring a coat. Unit problems are small, but they always arrive at the moment your hands are full.

I built the Unit Converter so the answer is two taps away, with no search page and no signal. It’s plain on purpose: one category, two units, one number.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says UNIT CONVERTER) and see a row of ten category buttons, a FROM box with a unit dropdown, a TO box with a unit dropdown and a RESULT panel. Type a number and the result updates straight away; there’s no “convert” button to press. A swap button flips From and To in one tap, and a QUICK row of buttons (1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100) fills in common values for you. Below the result, a line shows the rate, for example “1 KM = 0.6214 MILE”.

10 categories, 62 units

  • Length: km, mile, m, cm, mm, ft, inch, yd.
  • Weight: kg, g, mg, lb, oz, ton, stone.
  • Temperature: °C, °F, K.
  • Speed: km/h, mph, m/s, knot, ft/s.
  • Area: m², km², ft², acre, hectare, mile².
  • Volume: L, mL, gal, qt, pt, cup, m³.
  • Time: sec, min, hour, day, week, month, year.
  • Digital: B, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB.
  • Energy: J, kJ, cal, kcal, Wh, kWh, eV.
  • Pressure: Pa, kPa, bar, psi, atm, torr.

When you switch category, the units reset to sensible defaults, such as KM to MILE for length.

Numbers worth knowing

These are the conversions people look up most, and the tool gives the same answers:

  • 1 km is about 0.6214 miles, and 1 mile is 1.609344 km.
  • 1 kg is about 2.2046 lb, and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.
  • 20 °C is 68 °F, and 0 °C is 273.15 K.
  • 1 US gallon is about 3.785 litres, and 1 knot is about 1.852 km/h.
  • 1 GB is 1,024 MB in this tool.

How the results are shown

Results are rounded to suit their size: four decimal places for ordinary numbers, two for results over a thousand, and exponent notation for very large or very small values so they fit on screen. Temperature results are shown to two decimal places. If the input can’t be read, the result says ERROR.

Why it’s offline

All the conversion factors are stored inside the app, so the Unit Converter is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It’s ideal on a plane, abroad with roaming off, or in a shop with no signal.

Who it helps

Travellers, cooks, students, DIYers and builders, runners and cyclists switching between kilometres and miles, gamers and developers checking storage sizes, and anyone who moves between metric and imperial.

A few tips

  • Use quick values for halving and doubling. Tap 10, then change the units to see the rate in a different light.
  • Use swap to go back. It flips From and To so you can check your answer the other way.
  • Check which gallon you need. The volume units here are the US ones.
  • Read the rate line. “1 FROM = x TO” is a quick sanity check on any answer.

An honest limit

A few units are approximations. A month is counted as 30 days and a year as 365, so the tool doesn’t account for leap years or different month lengths. The ton is the US short ton (907 kg), and the gallon, quart, pint and cup are US measures, not UK imperial ones. Digital storage uses binary steps of 1,024, which is how many operating systems report it, but not how drive makers label capacity.

Frequently asked questions

What can the Unit Converter convert?

Ten categories with 62 units: length, weight, temperature, speed, area, volume, time, digital storage, energy and pressure.
