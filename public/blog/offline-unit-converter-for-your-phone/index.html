What Orbitika's Converter does

The Converter handles length, weight, temperature, digital storage and more. It is one of Orbitika's free everyday tools, and it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

When you will use it

  • Travelling: road signs in miles or kilometres, weather in Fahrenheit or Celsius, luggage limits in kilograms or pounds.
  • Cooking: recipes from another country use cups, ounces, millilitres or grams.
  • Shopping: a screen size in inches, a weight in ounces, a bottle in fluid ounces.
  • Tech: understanding why a phone's storage looks smaller than the number on the box.

The US and UK use different pints and gallons, which is a common source of mistakes in recipes and fuel costs.

Digital storage

8 bits make 1 byte. Storage makers count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes, 1 MB as 1,000 KB and 1 GB as 1,000 MB. Computers sometimes count in units of 1,024 (a gibibyte, GiB, is 1,024 MiB). That is one reason a 128 GB phone can show about 119 in some places, and the operating system and preinstalled apps also take space.

Tips

  • Check the unit before you convert: US and UK units share names but not sizes.
  • For cooking, grams are more accurate than cups.
  • Round at the end, not in the middle of a calculation.
