Water Tracker: A Retro Dot-Matrix Bottle That Fills as You Drink, Offline

4 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Water Tracker in Orbitika is a free tool for counting glasses of water. The goal is 8 glasses of 250 ml, which is 2 litres a day. Tap Drink to add a glass and watch a dot-matrix bottle fill, tap Undo to correct a mistake, and see the last 7 days at a glance. It resets at midnight and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Ask someone how much water they drank today and you’ll usually get a shrug and a guess. By mid-afternoon most of us are running on coffee and good intentions. The fix doesn’t need an app with accounts and streak shaming; it needs a way to make one tap per glass and see the result.

That’s the Water Tracker: a retro bottle made of dots that fills up as you drink, and a goal you can see at a glance.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says WATER TRACKER) and see today’s date at the top, for example “WEDNESDAY, 04 NOV 2026”, with a small “Resets in” countdown to midnight. Below it is a bottle drawn in dots, a counter such as “3/8” with “3 of 8 glasses”, and a line showing the millilitres, for example “750 / 2000 ML”.

  • DRINK adds one glass, and the bottle fills a little higher.
  • UNDO removes the last glass if you tapped by mistake.
  • RESET sets today back to zero, after asking “Reset today?” so you can’t lose your count by accident.

The goal

The goal is 8 glasses a day, with each glass counted as 250 ml, which makes 2 litres. A line at the bottom of the screen spells it out: “1 glass = 250 ml. Goal is 2.0 L per day. Resets automatically at midnight.” When you reach 8, the display changes to “*** GOAL REACHED ***”, the Drink key changes to DONE, and your phone gives a firm vibration.

Last 7 days

Under the bottle is a “Last 7 days” strip, with a letter for each weekday (M, T, W, T, F, S, S) and a row of dots showing how many glasses you had on each day. It’s a quick way to see whether the week has been steady or patchy. The tool keeps the last seven days of history for this view.

Resets at midnight

Each new day starts at zero by itself. The tool checks the date regularly and again whenever you come back to the app, so it rolls over even if it was open overnight. The count is saved on your phone, so closing the app doesn’t lose it.

Why it’s offline

The Water Tracker is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s no account or cloud sync. Your counts are saved on the phone, which is also why nothing is shared with anyone.

Who it helps

People trying to drink more water, desk workers who forget, runners and walkers, anyone starting a simple health habit, and parents helping a teenager build one. It’s a gentle tool: one tap, one glass.

A few tips

  • Tap as you drink. If you leave it until evening, you’ll guess, and guesses run high.
  • Use a glass you know. If your usual glass is about 250 ml, the count will be close.
  • Put a glass by your keyboard, and tap Drink every time you refill it.
  • Check the weekly strip on Sundays. It shows which days you tend to miss.
  • Pair it with the Focus Timer. Take a drink at every break.

An honest limit

The goal is fixed at 8 glasses of 250 ml, so it isn’t personalised to your size, activity, climate or health, and it isn’t medical advice. You can’t change the glass size or the goal, and the count stops at 8, so extra glasses aren’t recorded. It also doesn’t send reminders, so you need to open it and tap. If you have a medical reason to drink more or less, follow your doctor’s guidance.

Frequently asked questions

What is the daily goal in the Water Tracker?

Eight glasses of 250 ml each, which is 2 litres a day.

How do I add or remove a glass?

Tap Drink to add a glass and Undo to remove the last one. Reset sets today back to zero after asking you to confirm.

When does it reset?

Automatically at midnight. A countdown at the top shows how long is left.

Does it show my history?

Yes. A Last 7 days strip shows how many glasses you had on each of the last seven days.

Does the Water Tracker work offline?

Yes. Your counts are saved on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
