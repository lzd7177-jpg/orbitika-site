SOS light and SOS flasher: how to signal for help at night with your phone

22 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: to signal for help at night, flash the SOS pattern (three short, three long, three short) toward where people might see you. Orbitika's SOS Light does this with your phone's light, and its SOS Flasher shows large flashing text on screen. Always call emergency services first if you have any signal.

What the two tools do

  • SOS Light: sends the SOS distress signal using your phone's light.
  • SOS Flasher: shows large flashing text on the screen, so it can be seen from a long distance.

Both are part of Orbitika's emergency kit and are included in Orbitika Premium.

What the SOS signal is

SOS in Morse code is three short signals, three long signals, then three short signals. It is recognised internationally, and it works with light, sound or anything else you can switch on and off. A phone that repeats the pattern for you means you do not have to keep count while you are cold, tired or frightened.

When a light signal helps

  • You are outdoors at night and need to be seen by searchers or people far away.
  • You have no mobile signal, so you cannot call.
  • You are injured or cannot move around much, and want to signal without shouting.

Tips for using it well

  • Call first if you can. If there is any signal, call your local emergency number before anything else.
  • Go to an open, higher spot if it is safe to do so, so the light is not blocked by trees or buildings.
  • Point it toward where people might be, such as a road, a trail or a coast, and keep the phone steady.
  • Save battery. Signal in bursts when you think someone might see you, instead of running it constantly.
  • Do not shine it into the eyes of drivers or at aircraft. Bright lights can dazzle and create new danger.
  • Use other signals too. A whistle, a mirror in daylight or bright clothing all help.

What it does not replace

A phone light is a backup. It does not replace calling emergency services, carrying a proper torch or whistle on a hike, or telling someone your plan before you go.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SOS signal in Morse code?

Three short signals, three long signals, then three short signals. It is internationally recognised and can be made with light, sound or any other on-off signal.

What is the difference between SOS Light and SOS Flasher in Orbitika?

SOS Light sends the SOS signal using your phone's light. SOS Flasher shows large flashing text on the screen so it can be seen from a long distance.

Are the SOS tools free in Orbitika?

No. They are part of the emergency tools in Orbitika Premium, a one-time purchase of $4.99 (price may vary by country) with no subscription.

Can I rely on a phone light to be rescued?

No. Call your local emergency number first if you can. A phone light is a backup, and a proper torch, a whistle and telling someone your plan are better preparation.
