Travel Conversion Cheat Sheet: Km to Miles, Celsius to Fahrenheit, Kg to Lb and More

8 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Quick travel conversions: 1 km is about 0.62 miles, 1 mile is 1.61 km, 20 °C is 68 °F, 1 kg is 2.2 lb, and 1 US gallon is 3.785 litres. A fast mental shortcut for Celsius to Fahrenheit is to double it and add 30. The tables below cover the numbers travellers look up most.

The weather forecast says 30. The road sign says 80. The airline says your bag can weigh 23. Whether those numbers sound like a heatwave, a speeding ticket or a very heavy suitcase depends on which system you grew up with, and the answers are rarely at hand when you need them.

This cheat sheet gives you the conversions travellers look up most, in tables you can scan in a second, plus a few mental shortcuts for when you can’t get to a calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How many miles is 100 km?

About 62 miles. 1 kilometre is about 0.62 miles.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit quickly?

Double the Celsius number and add 30 for an estimate. The exact formula is °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, so 20 °C is 68 °F.

How many pounds is 23 kg?

About 50.7 pounds. 1 kg is about 2.2 pounds.

Is a US gallon the same as a UK gallon?

No. A US gallon is about 3.785 litres and a UK (imperial) gallon is about 4.546 litres.
