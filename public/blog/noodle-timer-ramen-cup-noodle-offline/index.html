Noodle Timer: 40 Korean and Japanese Ramen and Cup Noodle Cooking Times, Offline

2 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Noodle Timer in Orbitika is a free tool with preset cooking times for 40 noodles from Korea and Japan, from 2 to 5 minutes. Choose your noodle, tap Start, and it counts down with a progress ring and gives a strong vibration and a Ready message when it’s done. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Three minutes. Four and a half. Five. Instant noodles have a time printed on the packet, but it’s usually in small type, in another language, or on a wrapper that’s already in the bin. And noodles are one of the few foods where thirty seconds really does decide whether they’re perfect or sad.

That’s why I built the Noodle Timer. It knows the time for a long list of popular Korean and Japanese noodles, so you just pick yours and it counts down while you do something else.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says NOODLE TIMER) and see a dropdown that says “Select a noodle”, a countdown with a progress ring and the buttons below. Each noodle in the list shows its name, a small CUP tag if it’s a cup noodle and its cooking time. For Korean noodles, the Korean name is shown too.

  • Start begins the countdown, which shows as minutes and seconds with a status of “cooking…”.
  • Pause stops it (status “paused”), and Resume carries on.
  • Reset sets it back to the full time.

When the time runs out, the phone gives a strong vibration, the display says “READY!” and a pop-up tells you your noodle is ready, with an OK button to clear it.

40 noodles, two countries

The list has 19 Korean and 21 Japanese noodles, and 18 of the 40 are cup noodles. You can narrow the list with filter buttons: All, Cup and Packet, and a flag button for each country (🇰🇷 and 🇯🇵).

What’s in the list

  • Korean packet ramen: Soup Ramen (4:30, with Mild, Spicy and Bone at 4:00 and Beef at 4:30), Dry Ramen in several flavours (mostly 5:00), Potato Ramen (4:30) and Seafood Ramen (5:00).
  • Korean cup ramen: Cup Ramen in plain, spicy, kimchi, seafood and cheese varieties, plus Big Cup Ramen, all at 3:00.
  • Japanese cup noodles: Cup Noodle Original, Curry, Seafood, Chili Tomato and Tonkotsu, Big Cup Noodle, Maruchan Cup, and Super Cup Miso, Curry and Chili, all at 3:00.
  • Japanese ramen and yakisoba: Ramen in soy sauce, miso, tonkotsu and spicy (3:00), thin Seimen noodles (2:00) and Yakisoba and Spicy Yakisoba (3:00).

Times across the list run from 2 minutes (the thin Seimen noodles) to 5 minutes (dry ramen and Seafood Ramen).

Why it’s offline

All the noodles and times are stored inside the app, so the Noodle Timer is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It’s ideal for a dorm room, a campsite or a hostel kitchen.

Who it helps

Students, travellers, campers, late-night snackers and anyone who’s ever walked away from a pot and come back to mush. It’s also a good one for people who love Korean and Japanese noodles but can’t read the packet.

A few tips

  • Use the Cup filter for cup noodles. It shows only the 18 cup types, so the list is much shorter.
  • Prep before you start. Have your bowl, water and toppings ready so the timer reflects the real cooking.
  • Keep the app open while it counts down. The vibration and pop-up appear on the timer screen.
  • Taste a strand near the end. People like noodles firmer or softer, so adjust next time.

An honest limit

The times are presets in the app, not read from your packet, and you can’t set your own time. If your brand isn’t in the list, or a recipe gives a different time, choose the closest noodle or follow the packet. Always follow the instructions on your own pack for safety when handling boiling water.

Frequently asked questions

How many noodles are in the Noodle Timer?

Forty: 19 Korean and 21 Japanese, of which 18 are cup noodles.

What cooking times are available?

Times range from 2 minutes (thin Seimen noodles) to 5 minutes (dry ramen and Seafood Ramen). Most cup noodles are 3 minutes.

How does it tell me the noodles are ready?

With a strong vibration, a READY! message on the timer and a pop-up with an OK button.

Can I set my own time?

No. It uses the preset times for the noodle you choose, so pick the closest one or follow your packet.

Does the Noodle Timer work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
