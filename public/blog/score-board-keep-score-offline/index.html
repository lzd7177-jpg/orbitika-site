Score Board: Keep Score for Up to 6 Teams, Offline

2 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Score Board in Orbitika is a free tool for keeping score in games. It supports 2 to 6 teams with editable names, +1, +5, −1 and −5 buttons, a period counter, a game name and a score history. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Game night. Somebody says, “Wait, what’s the score?” and three people give three different answers. Paper gets lost, fingers don’t count past ten and arguments about who said what happen anyway. A big, clear score that everybody can see settles most of it.

The Score Board is made for exactly that: a big number for each team and a few buttons.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says SCORE BOARD) and see two team cards, TEAM A in orange and TEAM B in blue, each with a large score. Under each score are four buttons: −5, −1, +1 and +5. Every tap gives a light vibration, and a score never goes below zero.

Teams

  • 2 to 6 teams: tap Add Team for more, up to six, each in its own colour. You can remove a team once you have more than two.
  • Editable names: tap a team’s name to rename it; names are shown in capitals.
  • Exact scores: tap the big number and a “Set score” box opens, handy for correcting a mistake or starting from a handicap.

Game name and period

At the top you can name the game (it starts as GAME 1; type your own, such as “DARTS” or “QUIZ”). A Period counter has up and down arrows, so you can track the round, quarter or set you’re in.

Score history

Every change is recorded, and a Score History panel shows the latest ten entries with the team name and its new score. The tool keeps up to 50 entries, so you can see how the game unfolded and settle disputes about who scored last.

Reset and new game

The Reset button sets the scores back to zero. New Game returns everything to the starting setup of two teams, TEAM A and TEAM B, with the default game name.

Why it’s offline

The Score Board is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It runs on your phone, so it’s ready on a picnic blanket, at a campsite or in a hall with no signal.

Who it helps

Families on game night, quiz hosts, teachers running class contests, coaches and sports groups, and anyone playing darts, pool, cards or table tennis.

A few tips

  • Name the teams first. It’s easier to follow than TEAM A and TEAM B.
  • Use −1 for corrections. The history keeps a record of each change.
  • Use Set Score for handicaps. Start a team ahead or behind by typing the number.
  • Prop the phone up. Big numbers are easier to see from across the table.

An honest limit

The Score Board is simple. It adds in steps of 1 and 5 only, so a game that scores in other amounts needs a few taps or the Set Score box. It has no built-in game clock, no teams’ stats and no saved game list; the scores live on the screen while you use the tool, so finish and note the result before you leave it.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams can the Score Board track?

Two to six. It starts with two and you can add up to four more.

What buttons change the score?

−5, −1, +1 and +5 for each team. You can also tap the score to set an exact number. Scores never go below zero.

Can I rename teams?

Yes. Tap a team’s name to rename it. Names are shown in capitals.

Does it keep a history?

Yes. It shows the latest ten score changes and keeps up to 50.

Does the Score Board work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
