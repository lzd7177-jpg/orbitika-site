Sleep Sounds: Nine Ambient Sounds That Play Without Wi-Fi or Mobile Data

4 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Sleep Sounds is a free tool inside Orbitika with nine looping ambient sounds: Ticking Clock, Water Dripping, Sea Waves, Sound of Nature, Rain on Tent, Waterfall & Birds, Thunderstorm, Wind Blowing and Music Box. Tap one to play and tap it again to pause. The sounds are stored in the app, so it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Some nights the room is too quiet and every small noise becomes a big one. Others it’s the opposite: a neighbour, a road, a snoring seat-mate on a long flight. A steady, soft sound in the background gives your mind something dull to rest on. Many people swear by rain; others prefer a ticking clock.

I made Sleep Sounds so there’s no scrolling, no account and no streaming: nine sounds, one tap each, and they work on a plane or in a tent.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says SLEEP SOUNDS) and see a dot-matrix display at the top and a list of nine sounds below. The display says “TAP TO PLAY” until you choose something, then shows the name of the sound that’s playing. Tap a sound to start it, and tap it again to pause. If another sound is playing, tapping a new one switches to it, so you never get two at once.

The nine sounds

  • ⏱️ Ticking Clock: a steady metronome for deep sleep.
  • 💧 Water Dripping: gentle cave drips.
  • 🌊 Sea Waves: a calm ocean shore.
  • 🌲 Sound of Nature: birds and ambient forest.
  • ⛺ Rain on Tent: cosy outdoor rain.
  • 🦜 Waterfall & Birds: flowing water with forest birds.
  • ⛈️ Thunderstorm: heavy rain and distant thunder.
  • 🌬️ Wind Blowing: a soothing mountain breeze.
  • 🎵 Music Box: gentle lullaby melodies.

They loop

Each sound repeats continuously until you pause it, so there’s no silence or restart in the middle of the night.

The dot display

At the top, a grid of small squares slowly fills in, then starts over, with the word SLEEP cut out of the pattern. It takes roughly a minute and a bit to fill. It’s there as a calm, slow animation to look at, and it plays while you use the tool.

Why it’s offline

All nine sounds are audio files packaged with the app, not streamed. That’s why Sleep Sounds is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. If a sound can’t be loaded, the app shows a short “Error loading sound” message.

Who it helps

Light sleepers, travellers on planes and trains, campers, shift workers sleeping in the day, parents setting up a calm bedtime, and people who use background sound to study or read.

A few tips

  • Try a few before bedtime. The best sound is a personal choice, and you can find yours in a minute.
  • Keep the volume low. It should blend into the room, not compete with it.
  • Charge your phone or plug it in. Playing audio all night uses power.
  • Turn the screen brightness down. The dot display is dim, but a dark room is better.
  • Use it for focus too. Rain or a ticking clock works well with the Focus Timer.

An honest limit

Sleep Sounds has no sleep timer, so a sound plays until you pause it or close the screen. It has no volume control of its own, so you adjust it with your phone’s volume buttons, and you can play only one sound at a time with no mixing. It’s for relaxation, not a treatment for sleep problems; if you struggle to sleep regularly, speak to a doctor.
