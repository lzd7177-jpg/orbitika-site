Decision Maker: Add Your Options, Tap Pick and Let the Phone Decide

24 October 2026 · updated 29 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Decision Maker is a free tool inside Orbitika. Type your options or load one of six presets such as Yes / No or Beach / Mountain / City / Forest, tap pick, and after a short spin the phone lands on one at random. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

“Where do you want to eat?” “I don’t mind, you choose.” “No, you choose.” If that conversation sounds familiar, you already know the problem: nobody is really undecided, everybody is just reluctant to be the one who decided. A random pick removes the pressure, because nobody has to take the blame.

I built Decision Maker for exactly those moments. It’s the digital version of flipping a coin, except it works for more than two choices and you can see it think for a moment, which is half the fun.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says DECISION MAKER) and see a text box with the hint “Add option…”, a list of your options and a big pick button. Until you add something, the result panel says “Add options and tap pick!”.

  • Add an option: type it and press enter or tap add. It appears in a numbered list.
  • Remove one: each option has a small remove button, so fixing a typo is quick.
  • Clear everything: one tap empties the list and resets the result panel.

Six presets

If you don’t want to type, tap a preset and the options load instantly. The presets are:

  • Yes / No
  • Eat / Sleep / Work
  • Movie / TV / Game / Read
  • Beach / Mountain / City / Forest
  • Coffee / Tea / Juice / Water
  • Call / Text / Email

After loading one, the panel says “Tap pick to decide!”. You can still add or remove options afterwards, so a preset is a starting point, not a fixed set.

The spin

When you tap pick, the result panel says “Thinking…” and then flashes through your options quickly, about every 80 milliseconds, for roughly a second and a bit. Then it lands on a final choice, shown with a tick like “✅ Beach”, and your phone gives a firm vibration. While it’s spinning the pick button is locked, so you can’t start a second spin by accident.

The final choice is a fresh random draw from your list, not simply whatever the flashing stopped on, so every option has the same chance every time.

Why it’s offline

The Decision Maker is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. The random choice is made on your phone, so it works in a queue, on a road trip or at a restaurant with no signal.

Who it helps

Friends and couples who can’t agree on dinner, families choosing a movie, teachers picking a game for the class, anyone staring at a to-do list, and people who simply want a tie-breaker. It’s also surprisingly useful for solo decisions: when you’re torn between two things, how you feel about the result often tells you what you wanted.

A few tips

  • Use the gut-check trick. If you feel a pang of disappointment when it picks something, you’ve just learned what you wanted.
  • Add an option twice to weight it. The tool doesn’t remove duplicates, so putting “Pizza” in twice makes it twice as likely.
  • Edit a preset. Load Coffee / Tea / Juice / Water, remove what you don’t have and add what you do.
  • Pair it with a list. Use Takeaway Ideas to find a cuisine, then Decision Maker to choose between the places.

An honest limit

Decision Maker is pure chance. It doesn’t remember what it picked last time and won’t avoid repeats, so you could get the same answer twice in a row. Your list isn’t saved either, so if you leave the tool you’ll need to re-enter your options or load a preset again. It’s a tie-breaker, not an adviser: for big decisions, use it to learn how you feel, not to decide for you.

Frequently asked questions

How many options can I add to Decision Maker?

You can add as many as you like. Type an option and press enter or tap add, and remove any you don’t want.

Does Decision Maker have presets?

Yes, six: Yes / No, Eat / Sleep / Work, Movie / TV / Game / Read, Beach / Mountain / City / Forest, Coffee / Tea / Juice / Water and Call / Text / Email.

Is every option equally likely?

Yes. Each pick is a fresh random draw. Adding the same option twice makes it twice as likely.

Is my list saved?

No. If you leave the tool, you will need to add your options again or load a preset.

Does Decision Maker work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
