SOS Flasher: A Full-Screen Neon LED Text Board for Signalling in the Dark

7 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The SOS Flasher in Orbitika turns your phone screen into a full-screen neon LED sign. Choose a message (SOS, HELP, WELCOME, CONCERT or your own text of up to 30 characters), one of seven neon colours, Scroll or Blink mode and a speed, then tap Start Digital Board. Tap the screen once to close it. It is part of the Emergency tools in Orbitika Premium and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

A torch is great for seeing. It’s not great for saying something. If you’re stuck at the side of a road at night, trying to attract the attention of a passing driver, or standing in an airport arrivals hall hoping a friend spots you, a small light doesn’t carry a message. A big, bright word does.

That’s the SOS Flasher: a full-screen LED-style sign on your phone, with a message you choose, in a colour that glows in the dark.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says SOS FLASHER) and see a setup screen with four sections: Message, Neon LED Color, Display Mode and Speed. When you’re ready, tap START DIGITAL BOARD. The phone turns sideways, hides the usual controls and fills the screen with your message in big pixel-style letters. A hint says: “Tap screen once running to close”. Tap anywhere and you’re back on the setup screen.

Message

  • Four presets: SOS, HELP, WELCOME and CONCERT.
  • Your own text: type anything into the “Enter custom text” box, up to 30 characters. If you clear the box, the message goes back to SOS.

Seven neon colours

Super-bright red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue and white. The default is neon cyan, chosen because it looks very bright in dark places. Pick the colour that stands out most against your surroundings: red for a roadside, white for a crowd, cyan for darkness.

Scroll or Blink

  • SCROLL: the message moves across the screen like a marquee sign.
  • BLINK: the message flashes on and off, which draws the eye from further away.

Speed

A slider runs from SLOW to FAST and starts at the middle. You can change it before you start, and the display updates if you move it while it’s running. Slow suits long messages that need to be read; fast blinking is more attention-grabbing.

Feel and behaviour

Starting and stopping the board gives a firm vibration, so you know it registered without looking. While it’s running, the screen is in full-screen landscape mode, and when you close it, the app returns to normal portrait orientation.

Why it’s offline

The SOS Flasher is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. It only needs your screen, which is why it’s useful in the exact places you’d struggle with a signal: a remote road, a beach at night or a campsite.

Who it helps

Drivers with a breakdown at night, hikers and campers, boaters, and anyone who needs to be seen from a distance. It’s also fun for lighter uses: “WELCOME” for an airport pickup, “CONCERT” for a gig, or a custom name to help a group find you in a crowd.

A few tips

  • Turn the screen brightness up. The sign is only as bright as your phone’s screen.
  • Use Blink for distance and Scroll for reading. Short words blink well; longer messages suit scrolling.
  • Keep the message short. “HELP” is clearer from afar than a sentence.
  • Check your screen timeout. If your phone sleeps quickly, set a longer timeout before you rely on it.
  • Pair it with the other signals. The sirens add sound, and Location Share tells people where you are.

An honest limit

The SOS Flasher is a lit-up text sign, not a Morse code torch, so it doesn’t flash the international SOS pattern; it shows the word “SOS” in the style you choose. It has no sound, and a phone screen is far less bright than a dedicated flare or strobe, so its range is limited. It doesn’t contact anyone, so if someone is in danger, call your local emergency number first.

Frequently asked questions

What does the SOS Flasher do?

It turns your phone screen into a full-screen neon LED text board that scrolls or blinks a message you choose.

What messages can I show?

Four presets (SOS, HELP, WELCOME and CONCERT) or your own text of up to 30 characters.

How do I close it?

Tap the screen once while it is running.

No. The SOS Flasher uses the screen. The separate SOS light uses the camera flash.

Does the SOS Flasher work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
