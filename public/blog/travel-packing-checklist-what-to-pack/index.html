Travel Packing Checklist: What to Pack for Any Trip

8 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Packing is the part of travelling that feels easy right up to the moment you realise what you’ve forgotten. The mistakes are almost always the same: the charger, the adapter, the medication, the one document you can’t replace on the road.

The trick is to pack from a list, not from memory, and to keep the list short enough that you’ll actually use it. Here’s the one I use, organised the way I pack.

1. Documents: the things you can’t replace

  • Passport or ID, and check the expiry date well before you travel.
  • Tickets and boarding passes, saved on your phone and, if you like, on paper too.
  • Visa and travel insurance details, if your trip needs them.
  • Wallet and cards: a couple of ways to pay, kept in different places.
  • Copies of your passport and insurance, stored separately from the originals.

2. Electronics

  • Phone and charger.
  • Power bank: most airlines require these in your carry-on, not checked luggage.
  • Travel adapter: check your destination with the Plug & Voltage Checker, or look it up in the plug types guide.
  • Headphones: useful on planes and trains.

3. Toiletries and health

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste, deodorant.
  • Medications and a small first-aid kit. Keep prescription medicines in their original packaging, and carry them in your hand luggage.
  • Liquids: carry-on limits vary by country and airline, so check before you fill the bag.

4. Clothing

  • Underwear and socks, a little more than the number of days if you can.
  • Shirts and tops that mix and match.
  • A jacket or outer layer: even in warm places, planes and evenings can be cool.
  • Comfortable shoes you’ve already worn in.

Extras by trip type

  • Beach: swimwear, sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses.
  • City: a small day bag, a reusable water bottle and a portable phone charger.
  • Hiking and camping: sturdy shoes, a rain layer, a torch and a whistle. Read the Survival Guide before you go, and tell someone your route.
  • Cold weather: thermal layers, gloves and a warm hat.
  • Business: a smart outfit, a notebook and your chargers in one pouch.

Smart packing habits

  1. Lay it all out first, then put back a third of the clothes.
  2. Roll or compress soft items to save space, and use a small pouch for cables.
  3. Pack documents last and put them where you can reach them without opening your bag.
  4. Leave space for souvenirs. Keep a list in the Gift & Souvenirs tool so you don’t forget anyone.
  5. Do a final check at the door: phone, wallet, keys, passport.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most important things to pack?

Documents (passport or ID, tickets, insurance, cards), a phone and charger, any medication, and a travel adapter if you need one.

Should power banks go in carry-on or checked luggage?

Most airlines require power banks and spare lithium batteries to be carried in your hand luggage. Check your airline’s rules.

How many items are in Orbitika’s Travel Checklist?

Fifteen essentials in four groups: Documents, Electronics, Toiletries and Clothing.

Can I add my own items to the checklist?

No. The 15 items are fixed. You can tick them off and reset the list for a new trip.

Is the Travel Checklist free?

Yes. It is one of the free tools in Orbitika and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
