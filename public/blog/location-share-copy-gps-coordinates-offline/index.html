Location Share: Copy Your GPS Position into a Message, Even Without Mobile Data

4 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Location Share is a tool inside Orbitika’s Emergency kit. It gets your GPS position, shows your latitude, longitude and accuracy, and prepares a message with those numbers and a Google Maps link. Tap Copy Location, then paste the message into any messaging app. Getting a GPS fix does not need mobile data, and the tool is part of Orbitika Premium.

“Where are you?” It’s the first question anyone asks when you call for help, and often the hardest to answer. You’re on a trail with no name, a roadside with no signs, a beach that looks like every other beach. “Near a tree” doesn’t help a rescuer.

Your phone already knows exactly where you are, to within a few metres. Location Share turns that into a short message you can send to anyone, in two taps.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says LOCATION SHARE) and it starts looking for your position straight away. A status banner shows what’s happening: “Getting location…”, then “Location acquired” in green when it has a fix, or “No location” in red if it can’t get one.

  • Coordinates: a compact line shows your latitude and longitude to six decimal places, and the accuracy in metres, such as “±8.5 m”.
  • Message preview: a box labelled MESSAGE shows exactly what will be copied.
  • Copy Location: one button copies the message to your clipboard and confirms with “Location copied to clipboard!”.
  • Refresh: a REFRESH button in the top corner gets a new fix, useful after you’ve moved.

What the message says

The copied message is plain text that works in any app: “My Location”, then the latitude, longitude and accuracy, then a Google Maps link made from those coordinates. Even if the link doesn’t open for the person who receives it, the coordinates themselves can be typed into any map.

Why it works without mobile data

A GPS fix comes from satellites, so getting your position does not need Wi-Fi or mobile data. Everything in this tool is designed to work without them. The map link only opens on the other person’s phone if they have a connection, but the coordinates are readable either way.

Who it helps

Hikers and campers, drivers who break down on quiet roads, boaters, people meeting friends in a large park or festival, and anyone who needs to tell someone exactly where they are. It’s also useful for sharing a spot you want to return to.

A few tips

  • Allow location access. The tool asks for permission the first time, and without it there is no position to share.
  • Wait for a good fix. Under open sky the accuracy number is small; trees, buildings and canyons make it larger. Tap Refresh if it’s high.
  • Paste it into whichever app you use. A text message, a chat app or an email all work.
  • Use it with the emergency numbers. Call first with Emergency Numbers, then send your coordinates.

An honest limit

Location Share doesn’t send anything itself. It copies the message, and you paste it into a messaging app, so sending it still needs a mobile signal or Wi-Fi at that moment. If you have no signal at all, the message can’t leave your phone, though you can read the coordinates out to a rescuer or write them down. GPS accuracy varies, so check the “±” figure before you rely on it.

Frequently asked questions

What does Location Share do?

It gets your GPS position and prepares a message with your latitude, longitude, accuracy and a Google Maps link, which you copy and paste into any messaging app.

Does it send the message for me?

No. It copies the message to your clipboard and you paste it into an app yourself.

Does it need mobile data to find my location?

No. A GPS fix comes from satellites. You need a signal or Wi-Fi only to send the message.

How accurate is the position?

The tool shows an accuracy figure in metres, such as ±8.5 m. It is larger under trees, near tall buildings or in canyons. Tap Refresh for a new fix.

Is Location Share free?

No. It is part of the Emergency tools in Orbitika Premium, a one-time purchase with no subscription.
