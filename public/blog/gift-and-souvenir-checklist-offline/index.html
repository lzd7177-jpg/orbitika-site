Gift & Souvenirs: A Simple Offline Checklist for Presents You Still Need to Buy

29 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Gift & Souvenirs is a free checklist tool inside Orbitika. Type a gift or souvenir, tap the plus button to add it, tap an item to tick it off when you’ve bought it, and watch the counter show how many are done. The list is saved on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

It’s the last day of the trip. You’re in the airport, the gate is a ten-minute walk away and you suddenly remember that your sister wanted a magnet, your mum wanted something small and someone at work asked you to “bring back something local”. You open your notes app. The list is buried between a shopping list and a half-written email.

That’s the moment I wanted to fix. Gift & Souvenirs is a very small tool with one job: keep a short list of things to buy, and show you how many you have left. No accounts, no categories, no budgets. Just a list that’s quick to add to and easy to read in a hurry.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says GIFT & SOUVENIRS) and see a text box at the top with the hint “e.g., Gift for Mum, Souvenir magnet” and a plus button beside it. Type an item and either tap the plus button or press enter on the keyboard. The item appears in your list and the keyboard closes, so you can see the result straight away.

Ticking things off

Each item sits in its own card with a tick box on the left. Tap anywhere on the card and it’s marked as bought: the box fills in, the text gets a line through it and fades, and the card is dimmed so the things you still need stand out. Tap again if you change your mind. A small X on the right deletes the item.

The counter and the progress line

  • Your list counter: above the list you’ll see “Your list” and a count such as 5 out of the maximum number of items. When you reach the limit it turns orange.
  • Limit message: if you try to add another item at the limit, you get a “Limit reached” message and a stronger vibration instead.
  • Progress line: at the bottom it tells you how many you’ve bought, such as “3 of 5 bought”. When every item is ticked it changes to “All gifts bought!” with a tick icon.

An empty list shows a gift icon and the message “No gifts added yet.”

Saved between visits

The list is saved each time you add, tick or delete an item, so it’s still there when you come back. You don’t have to remember to press a save button.

Why it’s offline

Like everything in Orbitika, the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There is no sign-in and nothing to sync, which suits the places where you tend to need it: a market, an airport gate, a ferry or a street with no signal.

Who it helps

Travellers collecting souvenirs, people planning birthdays or Christmas, parents doing a last-minute class gift run, and anyone who tends to remember what they forgot to buy only after the shops have closed.

A few tips

  • Put the person and the idea in one line. “Dad: Pounamu keyring” is quicker to scan than two separate notes.
  • Add items the moment you think of them. The input box is the first thing on the screen, so it takes a few seconds.
  • Check the list before you head home. The “bought” line gives you a quick sense of how much is left.
  • Keep it for one trip or occasion. Delete finished items so the next list starts clean.

An honest limit

This is a deliberately simple checklist. Items are plain text, so there are no prices, no person field and no categories, and once an item is added you can tick or delete it but not edit it. If you misspell something, delete it and add it again. There is also a maximum number of items, shown in the counter, so it’s meant for a trip or an occasion rather than a life-long wish list.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add an item to Gift & Souvenirs?

Type it in the box at the top and tap the plus button or press enter on the keyboard.

How do I mark something as bought?

Tap the item. The tick box fills in and the text gets a line through it. Tap again to undo.

Is my list saved?

Yes. The list is saved each time you add, tick or delete an item, so it is still there when you return.

Is there a limit to how many items I can add?

Yes. The counter above the list shows how many you have used out of the maximum, and it turns orange when you reach it.

Does Gift & Souvenirs work offline?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
