Daily Walks Tracker: A Paw-Print Calendar for the Last 30 Days of Walks

1 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Daily Walks is a free tool inside Orbitika. Tap Log a Walk after each walk and it records the time, shows today’s count and how long ago the last walk was, and draws a 30-day paw-print calendar with your total, days walked and day streak. Your log is saved on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

“Did you walk the dog this morning?” “I thought you did.” In a lot of households that exchange happens daily, and the answer is a shrug. Nobody tracks walks because opening a full fitness app for something that takes one tap feels like overkill.

That’s the gap Daily Walks fills. It has one big button, a retro paw-print calendar and almost nothing else. Walk, tap, done.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says DAILY WALKS) and the top panel shows today’s date, for example “THURSDAY 29 OCT”, how many walks you’ve logged today (“TODAY x2”) and how long ago the last one was. That “last logged” readout counts up as the day goes: “JUST NOW”, “12M AGO”, “2H 05M AGO”, or “NONE YET” if you haven’t walked.

At the bottom are two keys: LOG A WALK adds a walk with the current time, with a firm vibration so you can feel it register, and UNDO removes the latest walk logged today if you tapped by mistake.

Three stats

  • Walks / 30D: the total number of walks in the last 30 days.
  • Days walked: how many of those 30 days had at least one walk.
  • Day streak: how many days in a row you’ve walked. If you haven’t walked yet today, the streak still counts up to yesterday, and only breaks when a day ends with no walk.

The paw-print calendar

The middle of the screen is a grid of 30 paw prints, one for each of the last 30 days, with the date range above it. A dim paw means one walk that day, a bright paw means two or more, and an empty slot means none. A caption reminds you: “Dim = 1 walk · Bright = 2+ walks · Tap a paw”. It’s a quick way to see your rhythm at a glance, such as good weeks, quiet weekends and gaps.

Tap a day for details

Tap any paw and a detail line shows that day, the number of walks and the time of each one, such as “07:30 · 18:15”. If nothing was logged, it says “No walk logged”. For days other than today, two extra buttons appear: + Add walk and − Remove. If you forgot to log a walk, you can add it afterwards, and it’s marked “MANUAL” instead of a time. Manual walks are filed at midday.

Saved on your phone

Your log is saved on the phone each time you add or remove a walk, so it’s there when you come back. The tool keeps about the last 35 days of walks and clears anything older, which keeps the 30-day view fast and tidy.

Why it’s offline

Daily Walks is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s no account and no GPS tracking, only a list of walk times saved on your phone, so it works at the park, on a trail or in the middle of nowhere.

Who it helps

Dog owners who share walking duties, families with kids doing the walking, people building a daily walk habit, and anyone who wants a streak to keep them honest. It’s also handy for a new puppy’s routine, where regular walks matter.

A few tips

  • Tap as you leave the house or when you get back, and stick to one habit so the times are comparable.
  • Log every walk. Two short walks make the paw bright.
  • Share a phone, or check it together. “LAST LOGGED” answers the “who walked the dog?” question in a glance.
  • Use + Add walk the same day you forget, so your streak and calendar stay accurate.

An honest limit

Daily Walks counts walks, nothing more. It doesn’t measure distance, steps, speed or duration, and it doesn’t use GPS. Because it only keeps about the last 35 days, it isn’t a long-term history. If you want steps or distance, Orbitika’s other tools cover that; this one is for the simple yes-or-no of “did we go?”.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a walk?

Tap Log a Walk after each walk. It records the current time, and you can tap Undo to remove the latest walk logged today.

What does the paw calendar show?

The last 30 days, one paw per day. A dim paw means one walk, a bright paw means two or more.

Can I add a walk I forgot to log?

Yes. Tap a past day’s paw and use Add Walk. It is marked as manual and filed at midday.

How long is my log kept?

About the last 35 days. The calendar shows the last 30.

Does Daily Walks work offline?

Yes. It saves your log on your phone and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
