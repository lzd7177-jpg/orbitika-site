Horoscope: Find Your Western and Chinese Zodiac Signs from Your Birthday, Offline

1 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Horoscope is a free tool inside Orbitika. Choose a day, month and year and tap Reveal My Signs, and it shows your Western zodiac sign (with dates, element, ruling planet and traits) and your Chinese zodiac animal (with years, traits, compatible and incompatible signs). It is for entertainment and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

“What’s your sign?” It’s one of the oldest icebreakers there is, and almost everyone knows their Western sign. But ask someone for their Chinese zodiac animal and you’ll often get a pause, a bit of counting on fingers and an “um, I think I’m a Rabbit?” I wanted one screen that gives you both in a second.

The Horoscope tool is deliberately simple. You give it a birthday; it gives you your two signs and what traditionally goes with them. It doesn’t predict your week, and it doesn’t need a signal.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says HOROSCOPE) and see a short prompt, “Enter your birthday to discover your Western & Chinese zodiac signs”. Under it are three dropdowns for Day, Month and Year, then a REVEAL MY SIGNS button. The day list adjusts to the month, including February in leap years, and the year list covers the last 100 years up to the current year. Until you tap the button, the panel says “Enter your birthday and tap the button above”.

Your Western sign

The result begins with your Western sign, its emoji and its date range. It also shows:

  • Element: Fire, Earth, Air or Water.
  • Ruling planet: for example Mars for Aries, Venus for Taurus and Libra, Mercury for Gemini and Virgo, and the Moon for Cancer.
  • Traits: a short line, such as “Bold, confident, and adventurous” for Aries or “Compassionate, artistic, and intuitive” for Pisces.

All twelve signs are covered, from Aries (Mar 21 to Apr 19) through to Pisces (Feb 19 to Mar 20).

Your Chinese zodiac animal

Below that is your Chinese zodiac animal, one of twelve: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Rooster, Dog or Pig. The result lists:

  • The animal’s years, for example 2024, 2012, 2000, 1988, 1976 and 1964 for Dragon.
  • Traits, such as “Powerful, charismatic, and ambitious” for Dragon.
  • Compatible and incompatible signs. Rat, for example, is listed as compatible with Dragon, Monkey and Ox, and incompatible with Horse.
  • A short description of what that animal is known for.

The combined line

The result ends with a one-line mix of the two, for example “Scorpio + Rabbit”, followed by both sets of traits side by side. It’s a nice little summary to screenshot and send to a friend.

Why it’s offline

All the sign data is stored inside the app, so the Horoscope is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s nothing to fetch, and you can enter any birthday you like, including a friend’s.

Who it helps

People who like a fun conversation starter, anyone curious about their Chinese zodiac animal, friends comparing signs, and travellers who run into zodiac culture in Asia and want to know what the animals mean.

A few tips

  • Try a few birthdays. Family and friends make the best test.
  • Use the compatible list as a conversation starter, not a rulebook.
  • Look at the year list. It also shows the other years that share your animal, which is handy for guessing someone’s age bracket at a dinner.
  • Screenshot the result. It fits one screen.

An honest limit

This tool is for entertainment. It gives traits and traditions, not daily or weekly predictions. One technical detail matters if you were born in January or February: the Chinese zodiac animal is worked out from your birth year only. The Chinese new year falls in late January or February and changes every year, so someone born before it may belong to the previous year’s animal. If that’s you, check against a Lunar New Year calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Horoscope tool show?

Your Western zodiac sign (dates, element, ruling planet and traits) and your Chinese zodiac animal (years, traits, compatible and incompatible signs and a description).

Does it give daily predictions?

No. It shows traits and traditions for your signs, not daily or weekly predictions.

How is the Chinese zodiac animal worked out?

From your birth year only. People born in January or February before the Lunar New Year may belong to the previous year’s animal.

Which years can I choose?

The last 100 years, up to the current year.

Does Horoscope work offline?

Yes. All the data is stored inside the app, so it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
