Offline emergency tools on your phone: what is in Orbitika's emergency kit

21 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Orbitika's emergency kit has eight tools: a first aid guide, sirens, SMS location, an SOS flasher, an SOS light, emergency numbers, a survival guide and an emergency translator with phrases in 14 languages. They are designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data and are part of Orbitika Premium.

The eight emergency tools

  1. First Aid: guides for CPR, bleeding, choking and more.
  2. Sirens: a siren, a tsunami alarm and other emergency alarms.
  3. SMS Location: shares your GPS location in a text message.
  4. SOS Flasher: large flashing text that can be seen from a distance.
  5. SOS Light: the SOS distress signal using your phone's light.
  6. SOS Numbers: police, ambulance and fire numbers.
  7. Survival Guide: basics for fire, water, shelter and more.
  8. Translator: emergency phrases in 14 languages.

Why offline matters for emergencies

The situations where you need an emergency kit are often the ones where the signal is weakest: a hike, a long drive, a trip abroad or a storm. Orbitika is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data, so guides and tools are there when the network is not. Sharing your location by SMS still needs a mobile signal to send the message, but it does not need mobile data.

A useful way to prepare before a trip

  • Open each tool once at home so you know where things are.
  • Check the emergency numbers for the places you are visiting. The European Union uses 112, New Zealand uses 111, the United States uses 911, and the United Kingdom uses 999.
  • Pick the translator language you are most likely to need and read through the phrases.
  • Keep your phone charged. The SOS light and sirens use battery quickly.

What it does not replace

Orbitika is not a replacement for emergency services or for training. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number first. The first aid guide gives general guidance, and a certified first aid course teaches you far more than any app can.

Are they free?

The emergency tools are part of Orbitika Premium, a one-time purchase of $4.99 (the price may vary by country). The rest of the app, including the music player and the 24 everyday tools, is free to use.
