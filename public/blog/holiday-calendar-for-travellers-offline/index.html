Holiday Checker: Public Holidays for 11 Countries, Offline (2026 to 2028)

24 October 2026 · updated 29 October 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Holiday Checker in Orbitika is a free tool that tells you whether today is a workday, a weekend or a public holiday, and lists upcoming public holidays for 11 countries (New Zealand, Australia, the UK, the US, Canada, Germany, South Korea, Japan, Singapore, France and Malaysia). The data covers 2026 to 2028 and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Nothing ruins a trip like walking up to a closed door. The bank is shut, the shop is shut, the museum is shut, and a local passer-by says kindly, “Ah, it’s a public holiday.” I’ve had that moment, and I wanted a way to avoid it that doesn’t need a signal or a web search.

The Holiday Checker answers one question: is today a normal day, and when is the next day off? It’s deliberately simple, because it’s something you want to know in five seconds.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says HOLIDAY CHECKER) and see a country dropdown at the top. Below it a “Today” card tells you the date and what kind of day it is, and below that is a list of upcoming holidays.

  • Today card: it shows the full date, and one of three results: the name of the holiday if today is one, “Weekend!” if it’s a Saturday or Sunday, or “Workday” otherwise.
  • Upcoming holidays: a list of holidays from today onwards, each with the month, the day and the holiday’s name. If today is a holiday, it’s highlighted with a thicker border.

11 countries

You can choose from New Zealand, Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Germany, South Korea, Japan, Singapore, France and Malaysia. The tool opens on the country your phone is set to, if it’s one of those eleven, and falls back to New Zealand if it isn’t. You can switch country at any time to check a place you’re about to visit.

Built-in data for 2026, 2027 and 2028

The holiday dates are stored inside the app for the years 2026, 2027 and 2028. Each country has its own list. A few examples of what’s in there:

  • New Zealand: Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, ANZAC Day, King’s Birthday, Matariki, Labour Day, Christmas Day and Boxing Day, with observed-day entries where a holiday falls on a weekend.
  • United Kingdom: Easter, the Early May, Spring and Summer Bank Holidays, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.
  • United States: Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.
  • Germany: Ascension Day, Whit Monday and German Unity Day among others.

Japan and Malaysia have the longest lists of the eleven, with dozens of dates across the three years.

Why it’s offline

The dates are written into the app rather than downloaded, so the Holiday Checker is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s no loading and no account. You can check a country from a plane, a train or a hotel with no wi-fi.

Who it helps

Travellers planning around closures, people working with colleagues in other countries, freelancers scheduling deliveries, students on exchange, and anyone who has ever turned up to a shut office. It’s especially handy if you split time between two countries, since you can flip between them in a tap.

A few tips

  • Check the destination before you book. Switch the country and look at the dates you plan to travel.
  • Look at the day after too. Many places close for a long weekend around a holiday.
  • Use it with the date tool. Count the days with Date Difference, then check the holiday list for anything in between.
  • Remember time zones. A holiday starts at midnight local time. See how to convert time zones for calls.

An honest limit

The Holiday Checker lists national public holidays only. It doesn’t include regional or local ones, such as a provincial anniversary day, and the data stops at the end of 2028, so after that the list will say there are no upcoming holidays. The dates are built into the app, so they’re only as current as your installed version. For an important trip or deadline, confirm the date with a local source as well.
