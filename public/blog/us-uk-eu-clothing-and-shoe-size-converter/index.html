Size Chart: Convert US, UK, EU and Japan Clothing and Shoe Sizes, Offline

24 October 2026 · updated 1 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Size Chart in Orbitika is a free tool that converts a US clothing or shoe size into UK, EU, Japanese, chest, waist or foot-length equivalents, for men and women. Type a size such as M or 9, tap Find, and a full size table is shown underneath. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

You find the perfect jacket on an overseas website. The size says “EU 48”. You’re a medium. Or a 38. Or maybe a 10? Sizing is one of the few places where the same person can wear four different numbers, and the answer on a shop-floor shelf is usually a tiny printed chart you can’t read.

I made the Size Chart so you can look up your usual US size and see what it becomes elsewhere, in one screen and with no signal.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says SIZE CHART) and see two toggles: Clothing or Shoes, and Men or Women. Below them is a “Lookup size” box that asks for your US size, with the hint “Enter US size (e.g. M)” for clothing or “(e.g. 9)” for shoes. Type it, press enter or tap FIND, and a result card appears showing the size and all its equivalents. Under that sits the Full Size Table for that group, which scrolls sideways if it’s wide.

Clothing

The clothing chart covers XS, S, M, L, XL and XXL for both men and women, with these columns:

  • US, UK, EU and JP sizes,
  • Chest in centimetres, and
  • Waist in centimetres.

For example, a men’s US M is shown as UK 38, EU 48, a Japanese L, a chest of 96–101 cm and a waist of 81–86 cm. A women’s US M is UK 10, EU 38, Japanese L, with a chest of 91–96 cm and a waist of 71–76 cm.

Shoes

The shoe chart covers US 6 to 13 for men and US 5 to 11 for women, with these columns: US, UK, EU, CM and INCH. For example, a men’s US 9 is UK 8.5, EU 42 and 27 cm (10.6 inches), and a women’s US 7 is UK 5, EU 37 and 24 cm (9.4 inches).

The centimetre column helps when a shop lists foot length instead of a size. If you type a half size such as 8.5, the tool picks the nearest whole size in its table, and if you type something that isn’t in the table it tells you which sizes to try.

Why it’s offline

All of the charts are stored inside the app, so the Size Chart is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. That makes it useful in the exact places you need it: a shop abroad with no signal, a market stall or a changing room.

Who it helps

Online shoppers buying from overseas, travellers shopping abroad, people buying gifts for relatives in other countries, and anyone who has to translate between sizing systems.

A few tips

  • Measure yourself once. Chest, waist and foot length are more reliable than a size label.
  • Use the full table. Compare your chest and waist with the ranges, not just the letter.
  • Between sizes? Size up. Especially for shoes, where a few millimetres matter.
  • Keep your own numbers. Once you know your US size, you can read straight across the table to UK, EU or JP.

An honest limit

Sizes vary between brands, and a conversion chart can only give typical equivalents. The lookup also starts from a US size: you can’t type an EU or UK size and get a US one, though you can read the full table in the other direction. The tool covers adult men’s and women’s clothing and shoes only, with no children’s sizes, and the shoe chart has a limited range (US 6–13 men, 5–11 women). If your size is outside the table, check the brand’s own chart.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Size Chart convert?

US clothing sizes (XS to XXL) and shoe sizes to UK, EU, Japan, chest and waist in cm, or foot length in cm and inches, for men and women.

Can I type a UK or EU size?

No. The lookup starts from a US size, though you can read the full table in either direction.

What happens if I type a half shoe size?

The tool picks the nearest whole size in its table.

Are children’s sizes included?

No. It covers adult men’s and women’s clothing and shoes.

Does the Size Chart work offline?

Yes. The charts are stored inside the app, so it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
