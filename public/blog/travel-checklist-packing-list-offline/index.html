Travel Checklist: A 15-Item Packing List You Can Tick Off Offline

7 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: The Travel Checklist in Orbitika is a free packing list with 15 ready-made items in four groups: Documents, Electronics, Toiletries and Clothing. Tap an item to mark it packed, watch the progress bar fill, and reset the whole list for your next trip. Your ticks are saved on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Everyone has a packing story. The passport that stayed on the kitchen bench. The charger that never made it into the bag. The adapter that was “definitely in there”. The scary part is that you don’t find out until you’re at the airport, or worse, at the other end.

The Travel Checklist is a short, calm answer to that. It’s a list of the things that are hardest to replace on the road, ready to tick off, with no setup and no signal needed.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says TRAVEL CHECKLIST) and see a progress panel at the top that says “Packing progress” with a count such as “6 / 15” and a bar that fills as you go. Below it the items are grouped under four headings. Tap an item and it’s marked as packed: the box fills in, the text gets a line through it and fades, and you feel a small tap. Tap again if you change your mind.

The 15 items in four groups

  • Documents: Passport / ID, Airline Tickets / Boarding Pass, Visa / Travel Insurance, and Wallet & Credit Cards.
  • Electronics: Phone & Charger, Power Bank, Travel Adapter, and Headphones.
  • Toiletries: Toothbrush & Paste, Deodorant, and Medications / First Aid.
  • Clothing: Underwear & Socks, Shirts / Tops, Jacket / Outerwear, and Comfortable Shoes.

The groups are in the order you’d probably pack them: the things you can’t travel without first, then the things that make the trip easier.

Saved on your phone

Every time you tick or untick an item, the list is saved on the phone. You can pack over several days, close the app and come back, and the ticks will still be there.

Reset for your next trip

A refresh button in the top corner has the tooltip “Reset for new trip”. Tap it and a box asks “Uncheck all? Ready for a new trip?” with Cancel and Reset buttons. Confirm, and every item is unticked, ready for next time. The confirmation means one stray tap can’t undo an hour of packing.

Why it’s offline

The Travel Checklist is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. The list lives on your phone, so you can use it at home, at the airport or in a hostel with no wi-fi.

Who it helps

Holidaymakers, students heading overseas, business travellers and anyone who packs in a hurry. It’s especially useful for a quick trip, when there’s no time to build a list from scratch.

A few tips

  • Tick as you put things in the bag, not as you think of them, so the list reflects what’s really packed.
  • Do the Documents group last. Pack passports and tickets just before you leave, then tick them.
  • Check the Travel Adapter with the plug tool. The Plug & Voltage Checker tells you whether you need one for your destination.
  • Add the extras to your head, not the app. Things like sunscreen or a swimsuit aren’t on the list, so make a note of your own.
  • Pair it with gifts. Use Gift & Souvenirs for what you’re bringing home.

An honest limit

This is a starter list, not a custom one. The 15 items are fixed: you can’t add, rename or delete items, and there’s only one list, so it doesn’t keep separate lists for different trips. Things like sunscreen, swimwear, a camera or a trip-specific item aren’t included. Use it as a safety net for the essentials, and add your own extras elsewhere.

How do I start a new trip?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
