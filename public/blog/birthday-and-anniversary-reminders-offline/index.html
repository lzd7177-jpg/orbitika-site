Reminders: A Simple Offline Countdown for Birthdays and Anniversaries

2 November 2026 · by the developer of Orbitika

Short answer: Reminders is a free tool inside Orbitika. Add a name, choose Birthday or Anniversary and pick the day and month. Your list is sorted by what’s coming up next, with labels like Today!, Tomorrow or In 12 days. It holds up to 10 reminders, saves them on your phone and is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Forgetting a birthday is one of those small failures that feels much bigger than it is. You remember at 9 pm, the shops are shut, and the card you meant to send is a text message with too many apologies. I wanted something that tells me, the moment I open it, who’s coming up next.

The Reminder tool is deliberately modest. It isn’t a calendar. It’s a short list of the dates that matter most, ordered by how soon they arrive.

What it does

You open the tool (the title bar says REMINDER) and see a name box with the hint “Name (e.g. Mum, Alex & Sam)”, a choice between Birthday and Anniversary, and a date button that says “Pick a date”. Tap it and a date picker opens (“Select date”). You choose a day and month, tap to save, and a message confirms the reminder was saved.

  • Birthday reminders show a 🎂 and Anniversary reminders show a 💍.
  • The date is just the day and month, for example “14 Mar”, so it repeats every year.
  • If you leave the name empty or forget the date, a short message asks you to fill it in.

The list

Your reminders are sorted so the next one to arrive is at the top. Each shows the name, the date and a label: Today!, Tomorrow or In N days. A counter beside the list shows how many you’ve used out of 10. Filter buttons let you show All, Birthdays or Anniversaries only, and an empty list says “No reminders yet. Add a birthday or anniversary!”.

Removing and clearing

Each reminder has a small remove button. A clear-all button removes everything, but first asks “Clear all?” and the number of reminders it will delete, so you can’t wipe the list with a stray tap.

Saved on your phone

The list is saved on your phone each time you add or remove something, so it’s still there when you come back.

Why it’s offline

Reminders is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. There’s no account and nothing to sync. The dates stay on your phone, which also means nothing is shared with a contacts list or a calendar service.

Who it helps

Anyone who wants a short list of important dates: family birthdays, a partner’s anniversary, friends’ milestones. It’s also handy for people who don’t want to give a calendar app access to their contacts.

A few tips

  • Add the dates in one sitting. Ten reminders take only a couple of minutes.
  • Use names that jog your memory. “Mum” or “Alex & Sam” are clearer than a first name alone.
  • Open it at the start of the week. The list is sorted, so the first item is what’s next.
  • Pair it with the gift list. When a date is close, add ideas to the Gift & Souvenirs checklist.

An honest limit

This tool doesn’t send notifications. It shows a countdown when you open it, so you need to check it yourself. It holds up to 10 reminders, covers only birthdays and anniversaries, and saves only a day and month, with no year, so it can’t work out ages. For alarms and alerts, use your phone’s own reminders alongside it.

What kinds of reminders are there?

Birthday and Anniversary. You pick a day and month, and it repeats every year.

How is the list sorted?

By the next date to arrive, with labels such as Today!, Tomorrow and In N days.

Yes. The list is saved on your phone, and the tool is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
