Travel is back on everybody’s list. Friends are booking trips they postponed for years, families are planning long-awaited holidays, and students are heading overseas. And almost every one of those trips has the same quiet problem: the moment you land, your phone’s best features depend on a connection you may not have, or may not want to pay roaming for.

I built Orbitika for exactly that gap. It’s an all-in-one app that’s designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data, and a surprising number of its tools happen to be travel tools. This guide walks through them in the order a trip actually happens: before you go, while you’re there, and if something goes wrong.

Before you leave

  • Travel Checklist: 15 essentials in four groups (documents, electronics, toiletries and clothing) to tick off, with a progress bar and a one-tap reset for your next trip.
  • Plug & Voltage Checker: pick your home country and your destination across 47 countries to see whether you need a plug adapter or a voltage converter.
  • Holiday Checker: public holidays for 11 countries through 2028, so you don’t arrive on the one day everything is closed.
  • Timezone: the current time in 56 places, to check when it’s a sensible hour to call home.
  • Unit Converter: 62 units, so kilometres, miles, litres and degrees stop being a guessing game.
  • Size Chart: US, UK, EU and Japan clothing and shoe sizes, for shopping online before you go or in a shop when you’re there.

While you’re there

  • Must-Try Food: ten local dishes for each of 36 countries, so you know what to order.
  • Takeaway Ideas and the Decision Maker: for the evening when nobody can decide where to eat.
  • Gift & Souvenirs: a simple list of presents to buy, ticked off as you go.
  • Location marks: save your car park, hotel or campsite and find your way back to it.
  • WiFi Signal: see how strong the hotel or café Wi-Fi really is before you settle in for a video call.
  • The offline music player, Sleep Sounds and 18 mini-games: for flights, trains and long waits.

If something goes wrong

These are the Emergency tools, part of Orbitika Premium. I hope you never open them on a trip, but they’re the ones I’d least like to be without.

  • Emergency Numbers: police, ambulance and fire for 50 countries, with a copy button.
  • Emergency Translator: 18 ready-made phrases in 14 languages, from “I need help!” to “Where is the hospital?”.
  • First Aid Guide: seven emergencies in five languages.
  • Location Share: copies a message with your GPS position and a map link to paste into any app.
  • Survival Guide and the SOS Flasher: for hikes and remote roads.

Quick guides

  • Travel packing checklist: what to pack for any trip
  • What plug adapter do I need? Plug types and voltage for 47 countries
  • Travel conversion cheat sheet: km to miles, Celsius to Fahrenheit and more

A simple pre-trip routine

  1. A week before: check the Holiday Checker and Plug & Voltage Checker for your destination, and read the Emergency Numbers for every country on your route.
  2. A few days before: open the Travel Checklist and start ticking. Copy your passport and insurance details somewhere safe, and tell someone your plans.
  3. The day before: charge your devices, load the music you want to listen to, and finish packing. Do the Documents group last.
  4. On the plane: airplane mode on, and everything in Orbitika still works.

Why offline matters when you travel

Roaming can be expensive, local SIM cards take time to sort out, and the places you most want help, such as a trail, a ferry or a rural road, are often the places with the weakest signal. Tools that are designed to work without a connection remove that worry. They’re also light on older phones, which many people take on trips precisely because they don’t mind if it gets dropped in the sea.

Who it helps

Holidaymakers, backpackers, students on exchange, business travellers, families visiting relatives abroad, and anyone who wants fewer apps to carry and fewer things to remember.

An honest limit

Orbitika is a toolkit, not a travel agent. It isn’t a map or a navigation app, it doesn’t book anything, and the translator is a fixed set of phrases, not live translation. Some tools are deliberately simple: the Holiday Checker lists national public holidays only, the Plug & Voltage Checker can be cautious about small differences, and the data is built into your installed version. For visas, entry rules and health requirements, always check official government sources, and for anything urgent, call local emergency services first.

Frequently asked questions

Is Orbitika a good app for travel?

Yes, if you want a set of offline tools in one app: a packing checklist, plug and voltage checker, time zones, holidays, size and unit converters, a food guide, and emergency numbers, translation and first aid. It is not a map or booking app.

Does Orbitika work without roaming or mobile data?

Yes. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Sending a message, such as your location, still needs a signal or Wi-Fi at that moment.

Which travel tools are free?

The Travel Checklist, Plug & Voltage Checker, Holiday Checker, Timezone, Unit Converter, Size Chart, Must-Try Food, Gift & Souvenirs and WiFi Signal are free. The Emergency tools are part of Orbitika Premium.

How much is Orbitika Premium?

$4.99 as a one-time purchase, with no subscription. The price may vary by country.

Does Orbitika replace a map or translation app?

No. It does not do navigation, and the Emergency Translator is a fixed set of 18 phrases in 14 languages, not live translation.
