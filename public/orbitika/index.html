Orbitika: the facts

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Orbitika is a free offline app for Android and iPhone. It combines a retro music player, location marks, emergency tools, everyday tools and mini-games, and it is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data, including on older phones.

The five parts of the app

Retro music player

Play the MP3 and FLAC files stored on your phone with Winamp-style visualizers and retro color themes. No streaming account is needed.

Location marks

Save a place on your phone and find your way back to it later, even without a data connection.

Emergency tools

Eight tools for when something goes wrong: a first aid guide, sirens, SMS location, an SOS flasher, an SOS light, emergency numbers, a survival guide and an emergency translator with phrases in 14 languages. These are part of Orbitika Premium. See what each tool does.

Everyday tools

24 free tools for common jobs: calculator, calendar with holidays by country, clock, unit converter, date difference, decision maker, discount calculator, stopwatch, timezone converter, reminders, ruler and level, height measure, star map, size chart, system info, sketch pad, score board, noodle timer and more.

Mini-games

18 small games, including Snake, Sudoku, Tic-Tac-Toe and Slide Puzzle, for passing time offline.

Why it works on older phones

Orbitika is built with older hardware in mind and runs on Android 8.0 and up. I test it on real older devices; results will appear on the blog.
