Orbitika

A free all-in-one app for Android and iPhone: a retro music player, location marks, emergency tools, 50+ everyday tools and 18 mini-games. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data, and it is built to run well on older phones.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

Free to download · Works offline · Android 8.0+ · iPhone X+

What’s inside

Retro music player

Plays the MP3 and FLAC files already on your phone, with 13 Winamp-style visualizers and 7 retro colour themes. No streaming account, no signal needed.

Location marks

Save a spot, like a parked car, a campsite or a hotel, and find your way back to it later, even without a data connection. How it works.

Emergency tools

Eight tools for when something goes wrong, from a first aid guide to a translator with phrases in 14 languages. Part of Orbitika Premium. See the whole kit.

Everyday tools

Calculators, converters, clocks, travel helpers, trackers and little decision tools for the moments you need an answer and have no signal.

18 mini-games

Small games for passing time offline, including Snake, Sudoku, Tic-Tac-Toe and Slide Puzzle.

Built for older phones

Runs on Android 8.0 and up and iPhone X and newer. I test it on real older devices; see the Old Phone Lab.

The eight emergency tools

  • First Aid Guide: seven emergencies in five languages.
  • Sirens: siren, tsunami alarm and emergency alarm.
  • SOS Numbers: police, ambulance and fire for 50 countries.
  • Emergency Translator: 18 phrases in 14 languages.
  • SOS Light and SOS Flasher: signal for help at night.
  • Location Share (SMS Location): copies a message with your GPS position and a map link, ready to paste into any app.
  • Survival Guide: eight topics in five languages.

They are a backup, not a replacement for calling emergency services.

Free or paid?

Orbitika is free to download and use. Orbitika Premium is a one-time purchase of $4.99 (the price may vary by country), not a subscription, and it unlocks the emergency tools and extra visualizers. The music player, location marks, everyday tools and mini-games stay free.

Why offline?

Most apps now expect a constant connection, a login and often a monthly payment. Orbitika is the opposite: the music is yours, the tools run on the phone and nothing waits for a signal. That helps most on older phones, on trips, and in the places where coverage is weakest. A GPS fix does not need mobile data either, so location marks keep working.

Questions

Does Orbitika need internet?

No. It is designed to work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Sending your location as a message still needs a signal or Wi-Fi at that moment.

Is Orbitika free?

Yes, to download and use. Premium is an optional one-time purchase of $4.99 (price may vary by country) for the emergency tools and extra visualizers.

Which phones does it support?

Android 8.0 and newer, and iPhone X and newer.

Can I play my own music?

Yes. The player plays MP3 and FLAC files stored on your phone.
