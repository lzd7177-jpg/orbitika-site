Orbitika

A retro music player and 50+ tools and games that work without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Built to run well on older phones.

Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Offline music player with 13 Winamp-style visualizers and 7 retro themes.
  • Location marks so you can save a spot, like a parked car or a campsite, and find your way back.
  • Emergency tools, including emergency translation in 14 languages (part of Premium).
  • Everyday tools and 18 mini-games for the moments you have no signal.

Who it is for

People with older phones, travelers and students who are often offline, anyone tired of subscription apps, and anyone who misses the MP3 player era.
